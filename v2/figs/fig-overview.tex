\caption{\textbf{System overview on one decode node.}
\stepn{1}~The capacity model turns an offline profile into the limits admission
must respect, and \stepn{2}~the admission controller assigns each request a tier
every step. Residents keep their KV in the paged HBM pool; a rider's KV arrives
from prefill into host DDR \stepn{3}, and the rider holds no HBM blocks. Both are
rows of \emph{one} forward pass: \stepn{4}~each layer's rider KV is streamed into
a rotating slot while the previous layer computes, \stepn{5}~attention reads the
two KV sources while every other operator runs once over the whole batch, and
\stepn{6}~the new token's $K,V$ is written back to the authoritative host copy.}
\label{fig:overview}
