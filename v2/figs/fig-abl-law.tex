\caption{\textbf{Per-step overhead $\Delta T$ against rider count}, one A100-80
(PCIe), Qwen2.5-32B, 16 residents. Points are measured; lines are the fit
$\Delta T = F + c\,r$, whose slope is $c$ and whose intercept is $F$.
(a)~Context 512: with the copy stream serialised, $\Delta T$ tracks the link
time; overlapped, it does not. The grey region is past one third of the knee, so
the overlapped slope there is an upper bound on $c$. (b)~Context 128, every point
below the knee: removing the bytes while keeping every launch leaves the slope
unchanged, so the transfer is hidden and $c$ is exposed host work. The two
filled/open series are repeats of one configuration. (c)~Context 128: a blocking
device-to-host copy per layer against the deferred write-back; the two lines are
parallel, so the write path is a fixed cost and lives in $F$.}
