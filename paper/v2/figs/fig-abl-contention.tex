\caption{\textbf{Copies into HBM slow the decode step's GEMMs in proportion to
their traffic.} A100-80, one decode step's weight GEMMs at Qwen2.5-32B shapes
(15.6\,GB of weights read at 1.49\,TB/s), timed while a second stream copies
into HBM at a controlled rate; a device copy counts its read and its write.
The slope is $\kappa = 1.06$ in units of HBM peak. Copies from the host over
PCIe cost about four times more per byte.}
\label{fig:abl-contention}
